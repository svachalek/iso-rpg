Trees, bushes, rocks and grass from KayKit - Forest Nature Pack 1.0 by Kay
Lousberg (https://kaylousberg.itch.io/kaykit-forest-nature-pack), CC0, see
LICENSE.txt. Only the models in use are here, copied from the pack's
Assets/gltf/ColorN folders (the eight colours share one texture and differ
in their UVs: 1 to 3 greens, 4 teal, 5 and 6 autumn, 7 red, 8 pink).
Loaded at runtime by scripts/tile_library.gd; the .gdignore keeps the editor
from importing them as scenes.
